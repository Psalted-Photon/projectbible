# Reference recording script
#
# Read the sentences below OUT LOUD in one calm, natural take — the way you'd
# read a story to someone, not the way you'd read the news. Save the recording
# as reference.wav (or .m4a / .mp3) in this folder.
#
# Aim for 1–2 minutes. A quiet room matters more than a fancy microphone.
# Lines starting with "#" are notes to you — don't read them.
# These sentences are chosen to cover most of the sounds in English.

When the sunlight strikes raindrops in the air, they act as a prism and form a rainbow.

The rainbow is a division of white light into many beautiful colors.

These take the shape of a long, round arch, with its path high above and its two ends apparently beyond the horizon.

There is, according to legend, a boiling pot of gold at one end.

People look, but no one ever finds it.

# A few plain, everyday sentences:

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog near the river bank.

She sells fresh bread and warm coffee at the small corner shop every morning.

Please call me back as soon as you finish reading the whole chapter.

Six thick oak beams held up the old roof through many long winters.

We drove through the mountains and watched the stars come out one by one.

# A few lines in the style the voice will read most often:

In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.

The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want.

Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and lean not on your own understanding.

Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called children of God.

And now these three remain: faith, hope, and love; but the greatest of these is love.
