book: Deuteronomy

card: How Deuteronomy Is Built
- 1–4 · Moses looks back: forty years, from Horeb to the plains of Moab.
- 5–26 · The law retold: the Ten Commandments again, then how to live in the land.
- 27–30 · The covenant renewed: blessings and curses, ending with “choose life.”
- 31–34 · Moses’ last days: Joshua takes charge, then a song, a blessing and Moses’ death on Mount Nebo.
> Speeches, not stories: most of the book is Moses talking to the new generation on the edge of the land, in the last days of his life.
> The Shema: “Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is One” (6:4) is still said daily by Jews. Jesus calls the next verse the greatest commandment, and answers all three temptations in the wilderness with words from Deuteronomy.
> The name: from the Greek for “second law,” the law given a second time.
