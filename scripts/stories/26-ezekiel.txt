book: Ezekiel

card: How Ezekiel Is Built
- 1–3 · The call: a vision of God’s glory by a river in Babylon.
- 4–24 · Judgment on Jerusalem: signs acted out, the glory leaving the temple, and the city’s sins laid bare.
- 25–32 · Messages against the nations, above all Tyre and Egypt.
- 33–39 · Hope after the fall: a good shepherd, a new heart, and dry bones that live again.
- 40–48 · A new temple: the glory returns, and a river flows out to bring life.
> The glory moves: Ezekiel sees God’s glory leave the temple (10–11) before Jerusalem falls, and watches it come back to a new temple (43). The book’s last words name the city “THE LORD IS THERE” (48:35).
> Acted-out messages: Ezekiel lies on his side for months, shaves his head and packs his bags for exile, turning his own life into the message.
> “Then they will know that I am the LORD”: the line runs through the whole book, more than 50 times.
