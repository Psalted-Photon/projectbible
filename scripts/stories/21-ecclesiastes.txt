book: Ecclesiastes

card: How Ecclesiastes Is Built
- 1–2 · The search: the Teacher tries wisdom, pleasure and hard work, and calls them all “futile.”
- 3–6 · Times and seasons: what lasts under the sun, and what doesn’t.
- 7–10 · What wisdom is worth, and where it runs out.
- 11–12 · Enjoy your days and remember your Creator before old age comes.
> “Under the sun”: the phrase comes nearly 30 times. The Teacher looks at life from ground level, the way anyone can see it.
> Futile: the Hebrew word means breath or vapor, something real but brief that slips through the fingers.
> The conclusion: “Fear God and keep His commandments, because this is the whole duty of man” (12:13).
