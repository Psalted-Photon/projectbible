book: Matthew

card: How Matthew Is Built
- 1–4 · Beginnings: the son of David and son of Abraham, born in Bethlehem, baptized and tested.
- 5–25 · Five great talks, with stories between them: the Sermon on the Mount (5–7), sending out the Twelve (10), parables (13), life together (18), and the end of the age (24–25).
- 26–28 · The cross and the empty tomb, ending with “Go and make disciples of all nations.”
> Five talks: each ends with nearly the same words, “When Jesus had finished…” (7:28, 11:1, 13:53, 19:1, 26:1). Many readers see an echo of the five books of Moses.
> “To fulfill what was spoken”: Matthew quotes the prophets more than any other Gospel, showing Jesus as the one Israel was waiting for.
> God with us: the book opens with Immanuel, “God with us” (1:23), and closes with “I am with you always” (28:20).
> “Also in”: stories Mark tells too have a line under them that opens Mark’s telling.
