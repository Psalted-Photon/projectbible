book: Acts

card: How Acts Is Built
- 1–7 · Jerusalem: the Spirit comes at Pentecost and the church grows, until Stephen is stoned.
- 8–12 · Judea and Samaria: Philip, Saul’s conversion, and Peter at the home of Cornelius, a Gentile.
- 13–20 · To the ends of the earth: Paul’s three journeys across Asia Minor and Greece.
- 21–28 · Paul on trial: arrested in Jerusalem, he appeals to Caesar and reaches Rome.
> The outline in one verse: “you will be My witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth” (1:8).
> Peter, then Paul: Peter leads the first half and Paul the second, and their stories mirror each other. Each heals a man who could not walk, and each is freed from prison.
> “We”: from 16:10 the writer starts saying “we.” Luke himself traveled with Paul for parts of the journey.
