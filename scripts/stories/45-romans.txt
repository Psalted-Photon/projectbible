book: Romans

card: How Romans Is Built
- 1:1–17 · Greeting: Paul, who has never been to Rome, introduces the gospel he preaches.
- 1:18–3:20 · All have sinned: Gentiles and Jews alike stand guilty before God.
- 3:21–8:39 · Made right by faith: Abraham, Adam and Christ, freedom from sin and life in the Spirit, ending with nothing able to separate us from God’s love.
- 9–11 · Israel: has God’s word failed? Paul’s answer ends in praise.
- 12–16 · Living it out: living sacrifices, love, the strong and the weak, and greetings to friends.
> The theme: the gospel “is the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes” (1:16).
> Built like an argument: Paul raises the objections himself (“What then shall we say?”) and answers them, as if a listener kept interrupting.
> Written from Corinth, most likely around AD 57, before Paul’s visit to Rome.
