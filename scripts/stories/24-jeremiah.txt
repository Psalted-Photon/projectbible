book: Jeremiah

card: How Jeremiah Is Built
- 1–25 · Warnings: Jeremiah’s call, sermons at the temple gate, and messages acted out with a linen belt, a potter’s clay and a broken jar.
- 26–45 · Jeremiah’s story: plots, prison and a muddy cistern, the scroll the king burns, the fall of Jerusalem and the flight to Egypt. In the middle, the Book of Comfort (30–33) promises a new covenant.
- 46–51 · Messages against the nations, from Egypt to Babylon.
- 52 · Jerusalem’s fall, told again as in 2 Kings 24–25.
> Not in time order: the chapters jump between the reigns of Josiah, Jehoiakim and Zedekiah. Most chapters say which king it was.
> The weeping prophet: Jeremiah tells God exactly how he feels. His complaints (11–20) are some of the rawest prayers in the Bible.
> The new covenant: “I will put My law in their minds and inscribe it on their hearts” (31:33), quoted in full in Hebrews 8.
