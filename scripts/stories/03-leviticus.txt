book: Leviticus

card: How Leviticus Is Built
- 1–7 · Offerings: five kinds of sacrifice, first for the people, then the priests’ side of each.
- 8–10 · Priests: Aaron and his sons are ordained, and Nadab and Abihu offer strange fire.
- 11–15 · Clean and unclean: food, childbirth, skin disease, mildew and the body.
- 16 · The Day of Atonement: the center of the book, once a year, for the whole people.
- 17–27 · Holiness: “Be holy, because I am holy,” worked out in daily life, feasts, Sabbath years and Jubilee.
> Why it’s here: the tabernacle is finished at the end of Exodus. Leviticus answers the next question: how can a holy God live among his people? No one travels in this book; it all happens at the foot of Sinai.
> “Love your neighbor as yourself” first appears here (19:18), in the middle of the holiness laws.
