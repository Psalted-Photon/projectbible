book: Hosea

card: How the Twelve Are Built
- Before the exile · Hosea, Amos, Jonah and Micah, while Assyria was rising. Then Nahum, Habakkuk and Zephaniah, as Assyria fell and Babylon rose.
- Around the fall · Obadiah, most likely, against Edom for gloating when Jerusalem fell.
- After the exile · Haggai, Zechariah and Malachi, among the people who came home.
- Joel · gives no date.
> One scroll: in the Hebrew Bible these twelve short books are a single book, “The Twelve.” They’re called the Minor Prophets only because they’re short.
> Hosea’s marriage: Hosea marries Gomer, who leaves him, and he buys her back (1–3). His broken marriage pictures God’s faithfulness to unfaithful Israel.
> “The day of the LORD”: the phrase ties the Twelve together, from Joel and Amos to Malachi’s last chapter.
> The last word: Malachi promises that Elijah will come before the great day (4:5). The Gospels open with John the Baptist.
