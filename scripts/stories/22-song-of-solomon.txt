book: Song of Solomon

card: How the Song Is Built
- 1:1–2:7 · Longing: the bride and her beloved find each other.
- 2:8–3:5 · Spring: he calls her to come away, and she searches for him at night.
- 3:6–5:1 · The wedding: Solomon’s procession and the bridegroom’s praise.
- 5:2–8:4 · Lost and found: a missed knock, a search through the city, and praise renewed.
- 8:5–14 · Love as strong as death.
> Voices: the poem is sung by a woman, a man and a group of friends. Many Bibles mark who is speaking.
> The refrain: “Do not arouse or awaken love until the time is right” closes three of the sections (2:7, 3:5, 8:4).
> How it has been read: as a celebration of love between husband and wife, and through the centuries also as a picture of God’s love for his people.
