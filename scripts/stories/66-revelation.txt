book: Revelation

card: How Revelation Is Built
- 1–3 · The risen Christ: John’s vision on Patmos and letters to seven churches.
- 4–5 · The throne room: worship in heaven, and the Lamb takes the scroll.
- 6–16 · Three sevens: seven seals, seven trumpets and seven bowls, with visions between them of a woman, a dragon and two beasts.
- 17–20 · The fall of Babylon, the rider on the white horse, and the final judgment.
- 21–22 · All things new: the new Jerusalem, where God lives with his people.
> Sevens: seven churches, seals, trumpets and bowls. Seven runs through the book as the number of completeness.
> Meant to be heard: “Blessed is the one who reads aloud the words of this prophecy, and blessed are those who hear” (1:3).
> Echoes everywhere: it hardly ever quotes the Old Testament outright, yet hundreds of lines echo Daniel, Ezekiel, Isaiah, Exodus and the Psalms.
> Garden to city: the Bible opens in a garden (Genesis 2) and closes in a city with a river and the tree of life (22:1–2).
