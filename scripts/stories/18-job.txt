book: Job

card: How Job Is Built
- 1–2 · The story opens: a blameless man loses everything, and three friends come to sit with him.
- 3–27 · The debate: three rounds of speeches, Job against Eliphaz, Bildad and Zophar.
- 28–31 · A poem on wisdom, then Job’s last defense.
- 32–37 · Elihu: a younger man speaks up.
- 38–42 · The LORD answers from the whirlwind, and Job is restored.
> Story around poetry: the opening and the close are told as a story. Everything in between is poetry.
> The question underneath: does Job fear God “for nothing” (1:9)? The friends insist that suffering must mean sin. Job knows his doesn’t, and demands an answer.
> No reason given: God never tells Job about the scene in heaven. He answers with the wonders of creation, and Job is satisfied.
> Running out of words: the third round breaks down. Bildad’s last speech is six verses, and Zophar doesn’t speak at all.
