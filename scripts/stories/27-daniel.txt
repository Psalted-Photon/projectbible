book: Daniel

# Chapters 1–6 are six court stories; 7–12 are Daniel's visions.

card: How Daniel Is Built
- 1–6 · Six court stories: Daniel and his friends stay faithful in Babylon, from the king’s food to the lions’ den.
- 7–12 · Four visions: beasts from the sea, a ram and a goat, seventy weeks, and the time of the end.
> Two languages: from 2:4 to the end of chapter 7 the book is written in Aramaic, the language of the empire. The rest is Hebrew.
> One message, twice: Nebuchadnezzar’s statue (2) and Daniel’s four beasts (7) both show four kingdoms giving way to a kingdom that will never end.
> Even the kings say it: “His kingdom is an eternal kingdom” (4:3), from the mouth of Nebuchadnezzar.
