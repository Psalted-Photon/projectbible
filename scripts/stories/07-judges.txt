book: Judges

card: How Judges Is Built
- 1:1–3:6 · Two openings: the land left unconquered, then the pattern spelled out.
- 3:7–16:31 · Twelve judges: Othniel to Samson, each round a little worse than the last.
- 17–21 · Two closing stories: Micah’s idol and the Danites, then the crime at Gibeah and a civil war.
> The cycle: Israel does evil, an enemy oppresses them, they cry out, the LORD raises up a judge, and the land has rest, until the judge dies and it starts again (2:11–19).
> The downward spiral: the judges themselves get worse. Othniel is spotless, Gideon ends with an idol, Jephthah makes a terrible vow, and Samson breaks almost every vow he has.
> “No king in Israel”: the closing chapters keep saying it (17:6, 18:1, 19:1, 21:25), and everyone does what is right in his own eyes. The book points ahead to the kings.
