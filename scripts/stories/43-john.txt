book: John

card: How John Is Built
- 1:1–18 · The prologue: the Word who was with God and was God becomes flesh.
- 1:19–12:50 · The signs: seven signs, from water into wine to Lazarus raised, with long talks between them.
- 13–17 · The upper room: Jesus washes his friends’ feet and talks with them the night before he dies.
- 18–21 · The cross and the risen Jesus: Mary Magdalene, Thomas, and Peter restored by the sea.
> Why it was written: “so that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God” (20:31).
> “I am”: seven sayings (the bread of life, the light of the world, the gate, the good shepherd, the resurrection and the life, the way and the truth and the life, the true vine) echo the name God gave Moses.
> Its own telling: most of John isn’t in Matthew, Mark or Luke. There are no parables, and Jesus goes up to Jerusalem for several feasts.
