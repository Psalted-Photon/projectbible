book: 2 Chronicles

card: How Chronicles Is Built
- 1 Chronicles 1–9 · Names: from Adam to the families who came home from exile, with the most room given to Judah, David’s tribe, and Levi, the temple tribe.
- 1 Chronicles 10–29 · David: the ark comes to Jerusalem, and David plans and gathers for the temple.
- 2 Chronicles 1–9 · Solomon: the temple is built and filled with glory.
- 2 Chronicles 10–36 · Judah’s kings: from the split to the exile, ending with Cyrus sending the people home.
> Written for the returned: Chronicles retells Samuel and Kings for people back from Babylon, asking what the story means for them now. In Hebrew it is one book, and it comes last in the Hebrew Bible.
> What it leaves out: the northern kings appear only when they touch Judah, and David’s sin with Bathsheba and Solomon’s fall aren’t told. The focus is the temple, worship and David’s line.
> Seek the LORD: kings who seek the LORD prosper and kings who forsake him fall, often within the same reign. “If My people who are called by My name humble themselves and pray…” (2 Chronicles 7:14) sums it up.
> “Also in”: stories told in Samuel or Kings too have a line under them that opens the other telling.
