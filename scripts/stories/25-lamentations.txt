book: Lamentations

card: How Lamentations Is Built
- 1 · The city weeps: Jerusalem as a widow with no one to comfort her.
- 2 · The Lord’s anger: what the siege did to the city.
- 3 · One man’s grief, with hope at the center: “His mercies never fail. They are new every morning” (3:22–23).
- 4 · Then and now: Zion’s people before the fall and after.
- 5 · A prayer: “Restore us to Yourself, O LORD.”
> Acrostics: chapters 1, 2 and 4 have 22 verses each, one for each letter of the Hebrew alphabet. Chapter 3 gives every letter three verses, 66 in all. Chapter 5 has 22 verses but no alphabet, as if the pattern finally breaks.
> Hope in the middle: the brightest words in the book sit at its exact center.
> Grief in order: the alphabet holds the sorrow from first letter to last, mourning completely without being swallowed by it.
