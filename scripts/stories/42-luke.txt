book: Luke

card: How Luke Is Built
- 1–2 · Two births: John and Jesus, told side by side, with songs from Mary, Zechariah and Simeon.
- 3:1–9:50 · Galilee: teaching, healing and calling disciples.
- 9:51–19:27 · The road to Jerusalem: ten chapters on the way, holding most of the parables only Luke tells.
- 19:28–24:53 · Jerusalem: the last week, the cross, and the risen Jesus on the road to Emmaus.
> The turn: “Jesus resolutely set out for Jerusalem” (9:51). From there the whole story leans toward the city.
> An orderly account: Luke tells Theophilus he has “carefully investigated everything from the beginning” (1:3). Acts is his second volume.
> Who gets welcomed: shepherds, Samaritans, tax collectors, widows and a lost son. Luke keeps showing how far God’s welcome reaches.
> “Also in”: stories Mark tells too have a line under them that opens Mark’s telling.
