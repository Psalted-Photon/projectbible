book: Hebrews

card: How Hebrews Is Built
- 1–4 · Better than angels and Moses: the Son is God’s final word.
- 5–10 · A better priest and sacrifice: Jesus, a high priest in the order of Melchizedek, brings a new covenant with one offering for all time.
- 11 · By faith: the long roll call, from Abel to the prophets.
- 12–13 · Run the race: endurance, discipline and a kingdom that can’t be shaken.
> “Better”: the key word. Jesus is better than the angels, than Moses, than the priests and their sacrifices, and brings a better covenant with better promises.
> Warnings woven in: five times the teaching stops to warn against drifting away (2:1–4, 3:7–4:13, 5:11–6:12, 10:19–39, 12:14–29).
> No name given: the writer never says who he is. It reads like a sermon written down, and calls itself a “word of exhortation” (13:22).
