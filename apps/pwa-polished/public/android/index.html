irisBible for Android

Every Bible, commentary, map and Read Aloud voice comes inside the app. Once it's installed, it works with no internet.

Download for Android

On a computer? Download it here, copy the file to the phone (or a thumb drive), and open it there.

What's new

The Android app is almost ready. Check back soon.

Installing it

  1. Tap Download for Android. If your browser asks whether to keep the file, keep it.
  2. When it's done, open it from the notification, or from Files → Downloads.
  3. If Android says it can't install apps from there yet, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.
  4. Open irisBible. The first time, it spends a few minutes unpacking everything, and you can read while it does.

Good to know

Updates come to you. When a new version is out, the app offers it and installs it, with nothing to download here again.

Your notes come along. Already using irisBible on the web? Sign in inside the app and your notes, highlights, journal and reading plans arrive.
