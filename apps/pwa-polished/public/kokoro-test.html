Screen-off endurance — the real test

Makes speech and plays it on a loop, exactly like reading a chapter. The point is to find out whether the graphics chip keeps working once your phone is locked in your pocket — and whether it slows down as the phone warms up.

  1. Tap start. Wait for audio to begin.
  2. Lock your phone and pocket it for 5 minutes.
  3. Come back, unlock, reopen this page.
  4. Results appear automatically — they survive a reload.
The tests below are slow on purpose. The processor rows can take 45–70 seconds per run, four runs each. The log updates as it goes, so if it is moving, it is working. Each gives up on its own after 5 minutes.

Will the app survive multi-core mode?

This page already runs with the security setting that multi-core needs. So it is a free rehearsal: if the things the app loads from other websites arrive here, they will arrive after the setting is switched on for real.
