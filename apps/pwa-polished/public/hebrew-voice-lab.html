Hebrew Voice Lab

Pointed Hebrew is turned into sounds by the rules on this page, and the sounds go straight to a voice. espeak's Hebrew is never used. German is the main test because it has almost every Hebrew sound (sh, kh, ts, the throat r, the glottal catch). The Greek and English voices are here to compare against. The sound boxes can be edited: move the ˈ to change the stress, or change any sound, then play it again.

Voices

Voices download once (about 60 MB each) and stay in this browser. A voice the app already has installed is used from there.

Settings

Changing the shva setting rebuilds the sound boxes, which drops any edits you made in them.

Try any Hebrew

Paste pointed text (with vowel marks). Cantillation marks are ignored. Mark a qamats qatan (the short "o") with ׇ (U+05C7) when the text leaves it as a plain qamats.
