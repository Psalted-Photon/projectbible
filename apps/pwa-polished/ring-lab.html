RING LAB

Click any word to open the ring on it. Sliders keep it open.

Ring

Radius is solved, not set: the innermost pair has to clear the line of text, and the seats have to clear each other. Radius nudge is added on top of whichever constraint won. Text size drives the line height, which is what makes the ring grow with the reader's font.

Pill placement

Drift slides the pill through its band: 0 hugs the word, 1 hugs the arc. The band is measured against the seats that actually sit over the pill at its width, so a wider pill gets a shorter band — past about 110px it starts answering to the low seats either side of the gaps instead of the high one at 12 o clock, and the room collapses. Watch the Fit block. Word + Strong's is faked here from a short lemma table; in the app only interlinear and Greek/Hebrew taps carry morphology, so on an English word that pill does not render at all.
