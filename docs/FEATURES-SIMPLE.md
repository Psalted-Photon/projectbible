# ProjectBible — Features (Simple)

## 1. Reading the Bible
- Continuous scrolling
- Verse layout: verse-per-line, paragraphs with numbers, paragraphs without numbers
- Word wrap
- Last place remembered
- Words of Jesus in red
- Section headings
- Footnotes
- Cross-reference markers
- Bold and italic
- Art icons
- Place-name underlines
- Themed titles
- Book introductions
- Book of Enoch (Charles 1917, Laurence 1821)

## 2. Getting Around
- Book picker — 66 books, 10 color-coded categories
- Chapter grid
- Unavailable books hidden
- Back button
- Tappable references everywhere
- Reference shorthand parsing
- Toolbar follows scroll position

## 3. Translations
- Translation picker
- Switching keeps your place
- Daily drivers per language and testament

## 4. Interlinear (Greek & Hebrew)
- Toolbar toggle
- Settings with live preview
- Presets: Minimal, Study, Scholar, Custom
- Layers: English gloss, transliteration, lemma, Strong's number, parsing

## 5. Word Study
- Greek/Hebrew: entry info, short definition, full definition, KJV usage, derivation, inflection forms, occurrences, related words
- English: dictionary definitions, plurals and variants, people recognition
- Morphology codes in plain English (Greek, Hebrew/Aramaic, STEPBible)

## 6. Bible Encyclopedia
- Full ISBE articles, offline
- More Info from selected text
- Overview, article with collapsible contents, verses, map
- Browsable A–Z with page turns and its own search
- Linked to the dictionary, topical index and people
- Powers place-name underlines

## 7. Commentaries
- 14 public-domain commentaries: NET Bible Notes, Adam Clarke, John Wesley, John Calvin, KingComments, A.T. Robertson, Albert Barnes, E.W. Bullinger, Family Bible Notes, Abbott, Thomas Aquinas (Catena Aurea), Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown, Charles Spurgeon
- Per-author color and badge
- Markers in the text
- Filter by author
- Open in its own pane
- Clickable references
- Anchor sync
- Checkpoints

## 8. Cross-References
- Treasury of Scripture Knowledge
- Toggle markers
- Tap a marker for connected passages
- Shorthand resolution

## 9. Search
- Quick search across Bible, Strong's, notes, journal, saved verses, characters, encyclopedia, commentaries
- Strong's number lookup (G26, H430, g0026)
- Collapsible grouped results, per-category caps
- Commentary search on Enter
- Advanced search: match type, must also contain, must NOT contain, proximity, plurals, synonyms, case sensitivity, pronunciation
- Per-option help

## 10. Highlights & Notes
- 7 colors: yellow, green, orange, red, pink, purple, blue
- Styles: marker, text color, underline (solid, dashed, wavy, boxed)
- Verse and single-word highlights tracked separately
- Notes on any verse
- Note markers
- Notes searchable
- Saved verses
- Tap a word, drag for a phrase, tap again to extend
- Selection menu: Dissect, Search, Map, Highlight, Save, Notes, Repeats
- Menu styles: ring (default) or popup

## 11. Repeated Words
- Track up to 7 words
- Colors: Amber, Sky, Mint, Rose, Violet, Coral, Teal
- Whole-Bible occurrence highlighting
- Toolbar pills
- Counts per book
- Highlight All
- Persists across restarts

## 12. Journal
- One entry per day, title and body
- Rich text editing
- References become links
- Calendar view
- Searchable
- Keyboard shortcut: J

## 13. Reading Plans & Progress
- Bible in 1 Year
- New Testament in 90 Days
- Gospels in 30 Days
- Chronological Bible in 1 Year
- Psalms & Proverbs
- Robertson Gospel Harmony (30/60/90/184 days)
- Custom plans: books, days, order (canonical, chronological, shuffled)
- Multiple plans at once
- Today's reading
- Tick off chapters or passages
- Jump to passage
- Rename, archive, delete
- Completion screen
- Catch-up: even spread or dedicated days
- Reading streak
- Total chapters read
- Reading history
- Calendar view
- Cross-device sync with merge

## 14. Maps & Places
- Map pane
- Places in the current verse
- Place search
- Historical layers by period or year
- Places by type
- Offline tiles
- Linked to the encyclopedia

## 15. Art
- Classical paintings tied to verses
- Icons in the text
- Art pane — all artwork or current chapter
- Search by title or subject
- Docks to the fitting screen edge

## 16. Read Aloud
- On-device AI voice, offline
- Standard (US English, ~64 MB)
- Compact (US English, ~30 MB)
- Opt-in downloads
- Install your own voice from a file
- Read this chapter aloud
- Pause and resume
- Jump to a verse
- Speed 0.8×–1.5×
- Auto-advance
- Continuous reading across chapters, with spoken announcements
- Controls in the toolbar wherever you are
- App icon spins while generating
- Lock-screen controls with verse counter
- Sleep timer — by minutes or end of chapter, fades out, never navigates
- Highlight the verse being read
- Drifting glow
- Read section headings aloud

## 17. Audio
- Pre-recorded chapter audio
- Play, pause, stop
- Seek
- Auto-advance

## 18. Panes & Windows
- Edge-drag to open a window, sized by drag distance
- Window contents: Bible text, map, notes, word study, commentaries, journal, art, encyclopedia, topical, people
- Up to 6 windows
- Resizable from the whole header bar, independent state
- Opens beside the text on a wide screen, beneath it on a phone
- Panes: Settings, Map, Packs, Search, Notes, Commentaries, Wake Alarm
- Panes dock left, right, or bottom
- Reader stays visible behind the Settings pane
- Portrait lock, optional rotation

## 19. Content Packs
- Everything as installable packs
- Quick install
- Advanced install from file or URL
- Voice packs
- Installed list with sizes and removal
- Rebuilt packs reinstall; delete frees the space
- Database statistics
- On-demand loading with progress
- Bundled packs extracted on first run

## 20. Account & Sync
- Optional account
- Sign up, change name, change password, reset password, delete account
- Syncs: notes, notebooks, verse and word highlights, saved verses, journal, reading plans, reading progress
- Syncs settings: theme, custom theme, Notes and Journal themes, timezone, default translations, interlinear, red-letter, section headings, art icons, themed titles
- Does not sync: font size, line spacing, verse layout, word wrap, rotation, update checks, tap-word menu style
- Offline change queue
- Collapsed duplicate edits
- Retry with backoff
- Live cross-device updates
- Reading progress merges
- Fresh install won't overwrite account settings
- Profile tabs: Reading Plan, Saved Verses/Notes, Journal, Settings

## 21. App Settings & Appearance
- Five collapsible sections with live summaries
- No Save button — changes apply instantly
- Themes: Dark, Light, Sepia, Auto, Custom
- Per-theme red-letter tuning
- Custom theme: 20 typefaces, free text and background colors, live preview
- 10 saved text swatches and 10 background swatches
- Typefaces normalised so switching doesn't change apparent size
- Separate Notes and Journal writing themes
- Slide-away editor toolbar
- Font size 12–32px
- Line spacing
- Verse layout and word wrap
- Display toggles: red letters, themed titles, art icons, place-name underlines, tap-word menu style, rotation
- Read Aloud settings
- Time zone
- Automatic updates
- Manual update check
- Clear cache
- Daily greeting and Verse of the Day
- Church calendar awareness
- Installable as a PWA

## 22. Wake Alarm
- Set time and days
- Opens on: continue where you left off, a fixed chapter, or your plan's next reading
- Works with the app closed (needs an account and a connection at the set time)
- Passage decided when it rings, not when it was set
- Test alarm button

## 23. The Study Library
- Four works: Dictionary, Topical, Encyclopedia, People
- Fixed tabs, greyed when a work has nothing
- One card — each work keeps its place when you switch
- A–Z rail with entry counts, empty letters dimmed
- Per-work search
- Star entries, recently read list
- Resumes where you left off within 30 minutes

## 24. Nave's Topical Bible
- Browse every topic A–Z, or search
- Numbered outline per subject
- Verses under each point, tappable and colored by book
- Bridged to encyclopedia, dictionary and people
- Included with the encyclopedia pack

## 25. People
- Browse everyone A–Z, or search
- Open a person from the text
- Homonyms offered as a list, and the one you pick stays open
- Family links: parents, children, spouses
- Pin a biography beside the reader
- Bridged to encyclopedia, dictionary and topical

## 26. Notes & Notebooks
- Verse notes in a book-by-book tree
- User-named notebooks holding free-form pages
- Drill-down layout that works at any panel width
- Bible references become links as you type
- Links wait until the reference is finished
- Links re-check and correct themselves as you edit
- Expand a reference to print the verse inline, uneditable
- Tap a link for go-there, expand, collapse
- Syncs to your account
